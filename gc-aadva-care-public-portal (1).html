Do I need an account to renew Lab Care?
No. Your device serial plus an email address is enough — you'll receive the invoice, confirmation and renewal reminders by email. You can create an account later to manage several devices in one place, but it's optional.
My serial isn't recognised — what now?
First check for typos (letters vs numbers). If it still fails, the device may simply be unregistered — register it above and coverage activates from your install date. If you suspect the product isn't genuine, use "Report a suspicious product": counterfeit dental equipment and materials are a patient-safety risk.
I bought my Aadva second-hand. Can I get Lab Care?
Yes — register the device with its serial and install details. Once verified, the device becomes Lab Care eligible and software version-ups unlock for it.
How do I pay, and is it secure?
Card (Visa/Mastercard/Amex), SEPA Direct Debit, iDEAL or Bancontact — processed via Stripe. You receive the invoice by email immediately; auto-renew (optional) always emails you 30 days before charging.
What happens if Lab Care lapses?
The device keeps working, but new software version-ups (like Dental System 2025) and priority support stop until renewal. Auto-renew with the 30-day email reminder is the easiest way to avoid a gap.
