GC's Glass Ionomer Cement (GIC) technology is the cornerstone of modern minimum intervention dentistry. Unlike composite resins, glass ionomers form true chemical bonds with tooth structure through an ionic exchange with hydroxyapatite — eliminating the need for separate acid etching. Developed and pioneered by GC since the 1970s, this technology spans restorative, luting, base/liner and preventive applications. The key differentiator is its active fluoride release, which continues to protect the tooth long after placement and can "recharge" when exposed to fluoride from toothpaste or rinses — making it especially valuable in high-caries-risk patients.

Key Clinical Insights
  • Chemical adhesion to enamel and dentin — no separate bonding agent required
  • Sustained fluoride release and recharge capability for ongoing caries prevention
  • Thermal expansion coefficient closely matching natural tooth structure
  • Moisture-tolerant placement — suitable for paediatric and field dentistry
  • Biocompatible, pulp-friendly chemistry ideal for minimum intervention protocols
