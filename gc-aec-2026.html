GC Academic Excellence Contest 2026

Challenge yourself and join the GC Europe Academic Excellence Contest! Open to undergraduate and postgraduate dental students at all universities — showcase your clinical skills and win a trip to the European final in Leuven, Belgium.

How to participate Key dates
📅 Post your case between 1 January – 10 June 2026 (before 12 A.M.) · European Final in Leuven: 18–19 September 2026
The contest

How the contest works

The contest is based on the photographic documentation of a clinical step-by-step case using G-ænial Universal Injectable and everX Flow — from national selection to the European final.

1

Document your case

Junior & Senior cases per university — only 1 case accepted per student.

2

Post & register

Post on Facebook/Instagram between 1 Jan & 10 June 2026 (before 12 A.M.) with signed Registration document & Declaration of Consent.

3

National jury

A national independent jury selects the best case in each category.

4

European final

National Junior & Senior winners present in Leuven, Belgium on 18–19 Sep 2026.

5

Winners

2 European winners in each category + 1 Facebook winner + 1 Instagram winner.

Who can participate

Two categories, one free kit per student

All universities can participate. Interested universities receive a free-of-charge kit for each student, delivered personally by a GC representative who explains the techniques and contest rules.

Undergraduate

Junior — Cusp by Cusp Technique in Posterior Class II Teeth

Your kit includes:

  • G-ænial Universal Injectable, syringe A2
  • everX Flow syringe, Dentin shade
  • G-Premio Bond
  • Clinical guide, articles & registration documentation
Postgraduate

Senior — Injection Moulding Technique in Anterior Teeth

Your kit includes:

  • Injection Moulding Kit: G-ænial Universal Injectable syringes A1, A2, A3 & Exaclear
  • G-ænial Universal Injectable, syringe JE
  • G-ænial Universal Injectable, syringe A02
  • G-Premio Bond
  • Clinical guide, articles & registration documentation
Please note: each university can submit cases, but only one case per student is accepted, with a maximum of 70 cases per region in total (20 junior & 20 senior).
Where & when

How to participate

1. Post your case
On the "GC Academic Excellence Contest" open group on Facebook or Instagram, mentioning your name and university and quoting "GC Academic Excellence Contest 2026".
2. Respect the deadline
Between 1 January and 10 June 2026, before 12 A.M. Cases not posted, or posted after this date, will not be considered.
3. Send to your GC representative
The case in the given PowerPoint format, plus the duly filled and signed Registration Form & Declaration of Consent.
4. Rally your friends
Invite friends to like your case on Facebook/Instagram — the most-liked cases in Europe win the social media awards.
The Declaration of Consent also covers the right to use pictures of participants taken during local & European ceremonies and events. If the document is not received before the end date of the competition, the participation will not be considered.

Case presentation templates

Document every step of your clinical case following the official PowerPoint structure for your category.

Junior (Undergraduate) — presentation structure
  1. Case description — anamnesis & age; chief complaint & patient expectations
  2. Pre-operative view — 1 occlusal view (optional: 2 side views in maximum intercuspidation)
  3. Description of treatment plan
  4. Shade selection
  5. Prepared tooth
  6. Matrix placement
  7. Bonding application
  8. Build-up of approximal wall
  9. Placement of everX Flow for dentin replacement
  10. Placement of G-ænial Universal Injectable occlusally using cusp-by-cusp technique (preferably each step in one picture)
  11. Finishing & polishing
  12. Final situation
  13. Occlusion check
  14. Final situation at recall
  15. Optional: postoperative x-ray
Senior (Postgraduate) — presentation structure
  1. Case description — anamnesis & age; chief complaint & patient expectations
  2. Pre-operative view — 1 frontal view with smile (optional: 2 side views)
  3. Description of treatment plan
  4. Wax-up creation (optional: impression/scan; creation of the silicone key)
  5. Shade selection
  6. Prepared teeth
  7. Bonding application
  8. Fitting of the silicone key
  9. Injection process with G-ænial Universal Injectable — isolation/moisture control; injection phase (indicate the used technique with pictures); light-curing
  10. Finishing & polishing
  11. Final situation (optional: occlusion check)
  12. Final situation at recall
Evaluation

What the jury looks for

A local independent jury evaluates the cases and selects the winner(s) per category. The same criteria apply at the European final.

Anamnesis description Quality of treatment plan Originality & complexity Marginal adaptation Color match & characterization Anatomical reproduction Texture Quality of final result Quality of pictures Use of characterization effects Presentation & didactics

The European final

Two days in Leuven, Belgium — 18 & 19 September 2026

Day 1 · Afternoon

Presentations & award ceremony

All national 1st prize winners present their cases (5–10 min max) to an International Jury, followed by dinner in Leuven and the award ceremony.

Day 2 · Full day

Master course with hands-on

Master course on direct restorations with state-of-the-art GC materials at the GCE Campus.
