Not sure where to start? Match the material family to the case

Choose a Glass Ionomer

  • High caries risk
  • No perfect isolation possible
  • Less cooperative patients
  • Temporisation needed
Reach for: Fuji II LC · Fuji TRIAGE · Fuji IX GP family

Choose a Glass Hybrid

  • Moderate aesthetic demand
  • Affordable long-term solution
  • Bulk-fill, no layering
  • Amalgam replacement
Reach for: EQUIA Forte HT · EQUIA

Choose a Composite

  • Good isolation possible
  • High aesthetic demand
  • High strength required
  • Long-term solution needed
Reach for: G-ænial family · Essentia · everX Flow

GC Workflow · Indirect Restorative

Overlay & onlay — from A to Z

1

Preparation

  • Core build-up & undercuts: everX Flow
  • Immediate dentin sealing: G2-BOND Universal / G-Premio BOND
  • Deep margin elevation: G-ænial Universal Injectable
  • Temporise: TEMPSMART / REVOTEK LC
2

Material selection

  • Compromised structure: Initial LiSi Block
  • Bruxism / younger patients: CERASMART 270
  • See the comparison table above
3

Finishing & painting

  • Standard polishing wheels
  • LiSi Block → Lustre Pastes ONE
  • CERASMART 270 → OPTIGLAZE color
4

Cementation

  • First choice: G-CEM ONE
  • Extra adhesion: AEP or G-Premio BOND
  • Sufficient retention? Step can be omitted
